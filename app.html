        Settings

        Voice assist

        While the camera runs, the microphone listens and on-device speech recognition types what you say — and Sotto learns your lips as you speak, growing the silent phrasebook. Turning this off releases the microphone immediately; silent phrases keep working.

        Pipeline self-test

        Feeds one synthetic mouth-movement segment through the segmenter and matcher. No camera involved, nothing gets calibrated, and the result is labeled as what it is.

        Starter pack

        Diagnostics

        A live HUD under the camera: thresholds, levels, and the last few routing decisions. Costs nothing while off. Copy report puts the same numbers on the clipboard as JSON — paste it when reporting a problem.
